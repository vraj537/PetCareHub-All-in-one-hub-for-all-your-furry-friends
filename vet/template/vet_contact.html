Vet Guidelines

Everything you need to know about PetCareHub 🐾

1. Request Management

When a client books an appointment, you must Accept or Reject within your schedule. Accepting triggers a 30-minute payment window for the client.

2. Uploading Reports

To mark an appointment as Completed, you must upload the Medical Report. Your consultation fees are credited to your earnings only after this step.

3. No-Show Policy

If a client fails to arrive, use the Mark Absent button. This issues 1 Strike to the user. After 3 Strikes, the system blocks their Cash payment option permanently.

4. Schedule Locking

After your initial login and schedule setup, you can only update your timings once every 12 hours. This ensures stability for active bookings.

5. Going Offline

To go offline, you must provide a reason. If you have active appointments during those dates, the system will cancel them and notify the clients.

6. Account Removal

To request account removal, you must verify your identity by entering your password. Your request will then be sent to the Admin for final approval.
